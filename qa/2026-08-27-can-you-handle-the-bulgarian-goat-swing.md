# Can You Handle the Bulgarian Goat Swing?

The kettlebell swing has been touted as the best exercise since the pushup—and for good reason. It torches calories, builds strength in your glutes and hamstrings, and improves your cardiovascular conditioning.

[the Bulgarian Goat Swing](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/4747437219.html)

But there’s one major problem with the move: Most people bend down with their torso, the way you were taught to touch your toes in grade school. But that strains your lower back, says Dan John, a strength coach based in Burlingame, California. What you really need to do is push your hips back, so you feel the exercise in your glues and hamstrings.

So John came up with the solution, an exercise he calls the Bulgarian Goat Belly Swing. (What’s with the name? Other people kept hitchhiking his ideas, he says, so he gave it the strange moniker as a sort of unofficial trademark.)

[the Bulgarian Goat](https://peplux.cc/category/ienhvsa)

It looks pretty much like a kettlebell swing, but with one catch: Instead of swinging a kettlebell, you place the bell on your stomach, just above your belly button. “This forces the abdominal wall to ‘push back’ during the move and will usually remind people to tighten their butt cheeks, which will protect your back,” John says. “You can do this move until your hamstrings get sore.” You’ll get all the benefits of the kettlebell swing, without the chance you’ll do the move wrong.
