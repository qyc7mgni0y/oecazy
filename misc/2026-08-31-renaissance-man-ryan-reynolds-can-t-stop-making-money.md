# Renaissance Man Ryan Reynolds Can’t Stop Making Money

Ryan Reynolds has been a Hollywood A-lister for nearly two decades now, with a multi-million dollar superhero franchise, luxury endorsement deals, and even a soccer team to his name. But the Canadian comic actor started out with small bit parts and worked his way up from there. Here's everything we know about Ryan Reynolds' career and net worth.

Reynolds' first on-screen acting role came in 1991, when he appeared in the teen soap Hillside, released in the United States as Fifteen on Nickelodeon. In a 2016 interview with Kelly Ripa and Michael Strahan, Reynolds recalled his $150-per-day paycheque.

"I remember we were paid $150 per episode. I still had a paper route. I would do my TV show, then I’d go home and do my paper route." he said. "For me, I thought I was, like, a gajillionaire. For $150 a day, it was like a dream come true."

That dream continued to come true, and Reynolds went on to enjoy a TV career in the Nineties: he played Harvey in the 1996 TV movie that would launch the show Sabrina the Teenage Witch, and had a guest spot on The X-Files that same year. From 1998 to 2001, Reynolds was one of the series leads in sitcom Two Guys, a Girl, and a Pizza Place (later rebranded and better remembered as Two Guys and a Girl).

He also has several lucrative business ventures.

Reynolds held a 10 percent ownership stake in Aviation Gin, and his involvement with the brand featured heavily in several of its tongue-in-cheek ad campaigns, until he sold his share of the company to Diageo in 2020 for a staggering $610 million, as reported by Vanity Fair. In 2023, Mint Mobile—of which Reynolds reportedly owned 25 percent—sold to T-Mobile for $1.35 billion, equating to an estimated $300 million payday for the actor.
