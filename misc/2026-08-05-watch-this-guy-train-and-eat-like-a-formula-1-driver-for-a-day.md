# Watch This Guy Train and Eat Like a Formula 1 Driver for a Day

Fitness YouTuber Will Tennyson is finidng out what it really takes to be a top Formula 1 driver. He plans to follow the exercise and diet plan that the McLaren racing team typically uses. It includes two intense workout sessions and a full regimen of healthy eating.

"A lot of people seem to think that drivers don't have to be in great shape because they're just strapped into a car and there's very minimal movement, but to be at the top of the sport you have to be in peak physical condition," says Tennyson.

He starts his day at 7 a.m. with a healthy breakfast that consists of oatmeal with blueberries, sunflower seeds and honey, plus a slice of toast with almond butter and green tea for a total of 550 calories. He also downs 400 mL of mineral water with lemon.

Next, he heads to the gym for his first workout of the day: a cardio-intensive HIIT workout.

"A typical race car driver's heart rate will be anywhere from 140 to 170 beats per minute, and it can even get as high as 190 to 205 beats per minute," says Tennyson. "So there's not really many sporting activities out there other than long distance marathon running that will keep your body temperature and heart rate that high for that long as F1."

He also notes that a driver can lose anywhere from 4 to 11 pounds of body fluid during a race.

For the workout, he is doing 10 30-second rounds of sprints on a treadmill followed, by 90 seconds jogging for recovery.

Five rounds in, he explains that drivers (like their cars) are expected to also be as light as possible, and that the reported average body fat of a race car driver is anywhere from 4 to 10 percent.

"That was brutal," he says as he finishes. He had an average heart rate of 148 beats per minutes for the 20 minute workout. "I have no idea how they maintain that heart rate for 90 minutes," he says.
