# This 6-Minute Bodyweight Workout Will Torch Your Abs

[Will Torch Your Abs](https://pipeprospect.com/category/pim7f)

Forget 8-minute abs.

Athlean-X expert Jeff Cavaliere C.S.C.S. breaks down an equipment-free ab workout that he says will help shred your six-pack in just six minutes (see how that works?). He provides a version of the routine that will work for any ability level: beginners do each bodyweight exercise for 20 seconds, intermediate for 30 seconds, advanced for 40 seconds.

If you're really looking to burn your core, Cavaliere also gives some options for a "torcher" series. You'll need a pullup bar and other gear for some of these moves—and some major strength to pull them off.

Cavaliere starts off with ab halos, which targets the lower abs. Lie on the floor with your legs raised together above you, and then lift your pelvis, completing a circular motion, changing direction with each rep. (Torcher option: hanging knee drives.)

[Will Torch Your Abs](https://pepgen.cc/category/jy6btf)

Next up is the backward 7, a bottom-up exercise similar to the mountain climber; from an upper pushup position, draw your knee up to trace a backwards 7 shape. (Torcher option: hanging windshield knee drives.)

[Bodyweight Workout Will Torch](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/4276735166.html)

The third exercise is the sidewinder, which involves lifting your bodyweight up into a sideways plank position, opening up the hips as you do so. "You're going to get that lower oblique working hard to lift your hip as high as you possibly can," says Cavaliere. For the next exercise, perform the sidewinder again, this time hitting the opposite side of the body. (Torcher option: hanging corkscrew left and right.)

The fifth move is the bear hop, which involves jumping from a plank to bear crawl position while keeping the knees off the ground. Next up is a top-down rotation move, the alternating thigh tap: while lying flat on your back, simply rotate your upper body in order to touch your opposite thigh and come back down. (Torcher option: plate-drag planks.)

The final move is the power up. "When you're the most fatigued, you're going to need the most help," says Cavaliere. "Here you get a little bit of momentum on your side; drive your feet in, lift your torso up and reach for the sky. Do this explosively; a little bit of momentum here is going to go a long way because of how tired you might be at this point." (Torcher option: situp elbow-thrust.)
