# Fans Are Concerned for Bruce Springsteen After He Fell at a Concert

There's just no stopping Bruce Springsteen. The rock'n'roll legend suffered a nasty-looking fall while playing a gig in Amsterdam on Saturday, face-planting on stage before rolling onto his back, prompting many fans present to express their concern on social media.

The "Born to Run" singer handled the potentially embarrassing moment—which has already gone semi-viral on TikTok—with good humor as his bandmates helped him to his feet, and proved that he's "Tougher Than the Rest." Springsteen appeared to bounce back from his tumble almost instantaneously, jokingly calling out "Goodnight, everybody!" before brushing off the mishap and carrying on with his performance.
