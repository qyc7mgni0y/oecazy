Chris Pratt: Confirmed Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 is on its way

From starring in Avengers: Infinity War to this summer's Jurassic World, Chris Pratt is one seriously in-demand dude. And his schedule isn't slowing down any time soon: According to Pratt's Instagram, the next installment of Guardians of the Galaxy is set to start filming in 2019.

In a post celebrating his co-star Pom Klementieff's birthday, Pratt wrote: "Happy Birthday @pom.klementieff whom many of you know as Mantis!!! She’s such a unique, talented and driven artist. Can’t wait to start shooting Guardians Vol. 3 with her next year! Hope you’re having a good day Pom!"
