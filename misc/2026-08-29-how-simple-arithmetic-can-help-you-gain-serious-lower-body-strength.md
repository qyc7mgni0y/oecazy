# How Simple Arithmetic Can Help You Gain Serious Lower-Body Strength

You count reps, but you probably never imagined yourself solving arithmetic problems at the gym.

[Can Help You Gain](https://glp1.99ht99.com/blog/8006641559.html)

But Men’s Health training advisor David Jack wants you to give it a shot with this brawn and brain game shown.

Related: THE 21-DAY METASHRED From Men’s Health: 30 Minutes, 21 Days, Killer Results

Here’s how it works: Hold an isometric split squat and roll a medicine ball back-and-forth beneath your front leg. Every time the ball passes underneath, perform a specific addition problem in your head. (Jack explains the exact math pattern to follow in the video above.)

Your goal is to add your way up from 1 to 51 while holding the split squat the entire time. Then, you’ll work your way back down to 1 using subtraction. Once you’re done, switch legs and do it again.

Related: 10 Cardio Exercises That Burn More Calories than Running

Why play this brain game? It works a little bit like listening to music while you work out, Jack says. Scientists have long proven that tunes can boost athletic performance. Your favorite playlist may distract you from your effort, allowing you to train for harder and for longer.

Doing mental addition does the same thing. You’re more focused on the math problem than on your burning quads.

Related: Normal-Sized Guys Who Are Freakishly Strong Tell You How They Did It

The game also challenges you to push yourself harder, says Jack. Without the math problems, you might stop after only a minute. But add the brain game to the mix, and you may continue to hold the exercise until you finish counting to your designated number. This increases your muscles’ time under tension, which ultimately helps build mass, strength, and endurance.
