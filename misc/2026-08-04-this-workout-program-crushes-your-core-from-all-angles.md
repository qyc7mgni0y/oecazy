# This Workout Program Crushes Your Core From All Angles

Crunches. So 1995. Planks? Child’s play. For decades, those were the staple ways of training your core, and you’d do them every day—with iffy and sometimes painful results.

Those old-school movements don’t unlock the true power of your core. A strong core actually involves a quartet of muscle groups (abs, obliques, lower back muscles, and glutes) working together to stabilize your spine and twist your torso. Train those muscles (and build total-body strength, too!) with this 30-minute situp-free core blast.

Do it four or five days a week; on all other days, go for a 20-minute run or power walk.

The Warmup

Cat-Cow to Thoracic Rotation

[Core From All](https://pepnex.cc/l1ub0zr91v/)

Start on all fours, core tight. Round your spine toward the ceiling, letting your head drop. Next, arch your back, pushing your belly toward the floor. Return to a flat-back position, then reach your right arm toward the ceiling. Return to the start; repeat on the other side. That’s 1 rep; do reps for 40 seconds. Do 3 sets.

The Workout

1. Elevated Plank Double-Explode Row

Start in plank position, your left forearm on a bench or box and a medium-weight dumbbell in your right hand. Row it to your rib cage explosively. Do 2 straight reps, pausing at the top on the second. Do 10 to 12 total reps. Do 3 sets per side; don’t rest between sets.

Eb says: “Own the plank first. Hips and shoulders must stay square to the floor.”

2. Half-Bench Single-Arm Paused Press

Lie face up on a bench with the right side of your torso, your spine, and your right glute off the bench, glutes and abs tight. Hold a medium-weight dumbbell in your right hand, directly above your shoulder. Lower the weight to your chest. Pause, then press it back up. Do 8 reps. Do 3 sets per side; don’t rest between sets.

Eb says: Squeeze your abs (b)and glutes on the off-bench side; it should be in line with the on-bench side of your body.”

Hold the end of a medium-weight dumbbell with both hands at shoulder height, core tight, feet about shoulder width apart. Bend at the knees, lowering your torso until your thighs are parallel to the floor. Pause and hold for 15 seconds. Stand up. That’s 1 rep; do 3 sets of 4.

4. Corkscrew Hollow Rock

Lie on your back, arms and legs extended. Press your lower back into the floor, lifting your shoulder blades and legs slightly. Put your left hand on your head and touch your left elbow to your right knee. Do10 hollow rocks from this position; do 3 sets per side.

Eb says: “Elbow and knee should never lose contact. Squeeze your obliques to make that happen.”

5. V-Up

Start in hollow-body position, your shoulder blades and legs a few inches off the floor and your lower back pressed into the floor. Raise your legs and reach your hands toward your toes. Return to the start. That’s 1 rep; do 3 sets of 15.

6. Wraparound Farmer’s Walk

Stand holding a heavy dumb-bell at your left shoulder with your left hand. Take 10 steps forward, then 10 steps back.Shift the dumbbell to your chest, gripping it with both hands. Take 10 steps forward, then 10steps back. Now shift the dumb-bell to your right hand, holding it at your hip. Take 10 steps forward, then 10 steps back. That’s 1 set. Do 3 sets per side; don’t rest between sets.

Eb says: “Make all the walks look the same. Tighten your abs so that your shoulders are never tipping to one side.”
